# AFTER THE LAST SERVER

> An archive of machines still waiting.

**AFTER THE LAST SERVER** is a small digital museum about interfaces that survived the worlds they were built to reach.

Not nostalgia.  
Not a history of “old computers.”  
Not a gallery of retro UI.

The subject is the moment when an interface still expects a future that no longer exists.

A button still says **Reconnect**.  
A launcher still offers **Update**.  
A contact list still knows how to show who is online.  
A map still describes a world whose servers have been shut down.

The machine has not changed.  
The meaning around it has.

## 01 — RECONNECT

The first room begins with one ordinary application window.

**Status:** `Disconnected`  
**Action:** `Reconnect`

The visitor may press it.

`Connecting…`

Then:

`Server not found.`

A small label nearby:

> The interface kept waiting after there was nobody left to wait for.

## Curatorial rule

Every exhibit must be more than a screenshot.

It should preserve or reconstruct a specific expectation encoded by an interface: a connection, a presence, an update, a destination, a community, a promised future.

The museum is interested in what remains when that expectation becomes impossible.

## Tone

Quiet.  
Sparse.  
No ironic “retro” styling.  
No nostalgia bait.  
No fake CRT circus unless an exhibit genuinely requires it.

Interfaces are the light source. Empty space does most of the talking.
